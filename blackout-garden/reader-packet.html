FRESH-READER ROUTE · NO COACHING

BLACKOUT
GARDEN

Play twice, alone. Do not look at the source or ask what the “right” route is. Each run takes about three minutes. Your only job is to decide what stays lit.

  1. Choose places.
    On each night, click up to three cards, then hold the night.
  2. Read dawn.
    Notice the cost, the dark windows, and what crosses into the next map.
  3. Finish Mill Yard.
    Read all three lines of the final dawn receipt before restarting.

Run one: careful

Try to leave enough charge for the last night. What did you sacrifice, and why?

Run two: spendthrift

Protect as much as you can early. What is different when you reach Mill Yard?

Two notes for us

After both routes, answer in one or two sentences each: When did you understand that the reserve was shared? And what did the final dawn receipt tell you that the map did not?
